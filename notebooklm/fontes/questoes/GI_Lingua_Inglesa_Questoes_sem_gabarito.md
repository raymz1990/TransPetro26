Digital twins are virtual replicas of physical assets, such as pumps, pipelines or entire refineries. Sensors installed on the equipment send data continuously to the model, which is then used to simulate how the asset behaves under different conditions. Engineers can, for example, test a maintenance plan on the virtual copy before applying it in the field, which reduces both costs and risks.
